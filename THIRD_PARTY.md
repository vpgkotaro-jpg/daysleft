License texts for tesseract.js and tesseract.js-core are in `vendor/tesseract/`. The QR library keeps its MIT notice in the file header.

No datasets are used. The three sample labels and the 12 evaluation images are synthetic, generated by `tools/make-samples.cjs` (fictional patients and pharmacy). The drug-name list in `js/parser.js` is a hand-typed list of common generic names.

Fact cited in the app and README: after the 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake a medical team found that 31 of about 300 people at one shelter would run out of oral medicine within 7 days (J Nippon Med Sch medical association journal 2024;20(3):156).

Dev-only: `tesseract.js` (npm) for `npm run eval`; Playwright for `e2e/` and `tools/make-samples.cjs` (Apache-2.0).
