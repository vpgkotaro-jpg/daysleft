      Safety and privacy
      
  
      
    
        
      
      • No medical advice. DaysLeft only does arithmetic: units on hand divided by units per day. It does not check interactions or doses.
        • 
      
      • OCR can be wrong. Blur, glare and curved bottles cause misreads. You confirm every field, and the app shows the exact text it read.
        • 
      
      • Your data stays here. Photos are read in your browser and then discarded. Medicines and the card are stored in this browser's localStorage. No accounts, no analytics, no server.
        • 
      
      • Works offline after the first scan, because the OCR model is served from this site and cached.
        • 
      
      • Why: after the 2024 Noto Peninsula earthquake, a medical team found that 31 of about 300 people at one shelter would run out of oral medicine within 7 days, and nobody knew who until they asked each person (J Nippon Med Sch medical association journal 2024;20(3):156).
        • 
    
      
    
      Built with help from Claude (Anthropic) as an AI coding assistant. OCR: tesseract.js (Apache-2.0). QR: qrcode-generator (MIT). Source and licenses are in the repository.
